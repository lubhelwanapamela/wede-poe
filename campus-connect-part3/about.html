Who we are

Making student life simpler, one click at a time.

Campus Connect helps students find helpful information on academic support, student life, campus events and studying, all in one location.

Our story

Campus Connect was founded to make crucial student information easier to access. Instead of browsing through numerous websites and social media pages, students can find what they need in one place.

Our main aim is to help students feel supported and connected while they study. We share details about events, study materials, support services and other initiatives that improve the educational experience.

Mission statement

To give students quick access to helpful tools and information so they can enjoy their time as students and achieve academic success.

Vision statement

To become a reliable online resource where students can quickly locate information, connect with one another and get help whenever they need it.

Who we serve

College and university students, especially those aged 18 to 30. New students who need help finding information about campus programmes and events benefit too.

What this website is here to do
